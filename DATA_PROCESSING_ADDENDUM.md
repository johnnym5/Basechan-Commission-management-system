# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

*Effective Date: October 2026*

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between Basechan International ("Processor" or "Basechan") and partner organizations or institutional users ("Controller") using Basechan CMS to process commission and student routing data.

## 1. Scope & Roles

- **Data Controller:** The partner agency or institution determining the purposes and means of processing applicant and staff routing data.
- **Data Processor:** Basechan International, processing personal and operational data on behalf of the Controller through Basechan CMS.
- **Subject Matter:** Management of educational intake commission structures, agent rates, and staff application routing records.

## 2. Compliance with Data Protection Laws

Both parties agree to comply with applicable Data Protection Laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

## 3. Processor Obligations

Basechan International agrees to:

- Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller or as required by applicable law.
- Ensure all personnel authorized to process data are bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
- Implement technical and organizational measures to safeguard data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
- Assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, data export).

## 4. Subprocessors

- The Controller authorizes Basechan International to engage third-party subprocessors for database hosting, authentication, and system infrastructure.
- The active subprocessor list is published in [`SUBPROCESSORS.md`](file:///C:/Users/HP/Documents/CODING/BASECHAN%20COMMISSION%20MANAGEMENT%20SYSTEM/SUBPROCESSORS.md).
- Basechan will notify Controllers at least 14 days in advance of changes to subprocessors.

## 5. Security Incident Notification

In the event of a confirmed security incident or personal data breach affecting Controller data:

- Basechan will notify the Controller without undue delay and no later than **72 hours** after becoming aware of the incident.
- The notification will detail the nature of the breach, affected records, and remediation steps taken.

## 6. Data Return & Deletion

Upon termination of system access, Basechan shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all Controller personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by applicable law.
