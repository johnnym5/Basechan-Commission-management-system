# Basechan CMS Privacy Policy

*Effective Date: October 2026*

Basechan International ("Basechan", "we", "our") operates Basechan CMS. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and what rights you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to authenticate users and operate the commission system:

- **Account Data:** When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, avatar photo, and Google user ID. Sign-in is restricted to authorized email domains (such as `@basechaninternational.com` or invited partner domains).
- **System Activity & Logs:** We record sign-in timestamps, assigned user roles (Admin, Staff, Agent), active session status, and audit logs of rate edits or bulk Excel uploads.
- **Uploaded Documents:** Excel files and commission tables uploaded to the system contain institutional commission rates and intake details. They do not contain applicant end-user financial records unless intentionally submitted.
- **Cookies & Local Storage:** We use local storage to save your theme preferences (Light/Dark/System) and cookie consent choices. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

- Verify identity and enforce domain-based access security.
- Manage user authorization levels (Admin, Staff, Agent).
- Display commission schedules, university application routing rules, and agent rate comparisons.
- Maintain operational security and audit trails for database modifications.

## 3. AI & Data Training Policy

**We do not train AI models on user data.** 

Your uploaded Excel files, commission figures, notes, user profiles, and activity records are never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate public or proprietary AI or machine learning models.

## 4. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the essential service providers that host our system:

- **Google Cloud Platform / Firebase:** Database storage (Firestore), web hosting (Firebase Hosting), and user authentication (Firebase Auth).
- **Google Identity Services:** Google OAuth single sign-on.

See our [Subprocessors List](file:///C:/Users/HP/Documents/CODING/BASECHAN%20COMMISSION%20MANAGEMENT%20SYSTEM/SUBPROCESSORS.md) for full details.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- **Account Data:** Retained as long as your account remains active.
- **Data Export:** You can export a copy of your personal user profile and activity records in JSON format at any time using the in-app privacy controls.
- **Account Deletion:** You may request account deletion at any time. When deleted, your profile record is removed from Firestore within 30 days.

## 6. Your Rights

Under applicable privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:

- Access and request a copy of your personal data.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Delete your account and personal records ("Right to be Forgotten").
- Restrict or object to processing.
- Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app Privacy & Data Control panel or contact the Basechan Administrator.
